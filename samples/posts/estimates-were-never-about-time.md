title: Estimates were never about time
date: 2026-06-09
---

When a team is asked how long something will take, the question is rarely
about the calendar. It is a polite way of asking how sure they are.

"Three weeks" means *we understand this*. "Three months" means *we don't, and
we would like you to notice*. The number is a confidence interval dressed as a
date, and treating it as a promise punishes the only honest signal in the
room.
