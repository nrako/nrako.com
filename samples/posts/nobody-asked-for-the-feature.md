Every feature request is a solution wearing the costume of a problem. "Can you
add an export button?" is not about buttons. Somewhere, a person is retyping
numbers into a spreadsheet every Friday, and they have guessed at what would
make it stop.

## Ask what happened last time

The question that works is never "what do you need?". People answer it with
the feature they already imagined. The one that works is:

> Tell me about the last time this was a problem.

Then you get a Friday, a spreadsheet, a manager who wants the totals by noon,
and a detail nobody would have thought to mention: the numbers are retyped
because two of them are always corrected by hand first.

An export button would have shipped the wrong numbers faster.

## The distance

Between the request and the need there is usually one sentence the person did
not say because it seemed too obvious. Research is mostly the work of getting
that sentence said out loud.
